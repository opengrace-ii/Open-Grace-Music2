# YouTube: exact video only

OGM records **the audio from the URL you paste** — never a “similar” track from another site.

## How it works now

**Android APK 1.3.5+:** play the video, tap **Record**. The phone captures `googlevideo.com` audio for that video ID (optional Connect YouTube). No screen share.

**Website / PWA:** Record is a job for any online **recorder worker** (yt-dlp + ffmpeg on a residential IP). If no worker is online: **Open the APK to record on this phone.**

Public Invidious/Piped, Render yt-dlp, TV OAuth-for-download, and tab share are **removed**.
