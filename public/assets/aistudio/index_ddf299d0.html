Import from direct URL

Paste a direct link to an audio or video file (ends in .mp3, .m4a, .ogg, .wav, .flac, .webm, .mp4 …).
YouTube pages are not media files and can't be imported here — for videos you uploaded, download them from YouTube Studio first.

Install OGM on your iPhone / iPad

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. OGM opens fullscreen like a native app.
